## Full description (max 4000)

<!-- completa -->
Does your favourite game only have touch controls? Gamepad Mapper Plutón turns the buttons and sticks of any Bluetooth or USB gamepad into screen touches, exactly where you choose.

NO ROOT, NO PC
• Works with Android's accessibility service: turn it on once and you're done.
• Optional fast mode with Shizuku (lower latency) and optional root mode with Magisk.

PER-GAME PROFILES
• A floating bubble inside the game to create, edit and switch profiles without leaving it.
• Place each button over the game's touch control; the app finds the game's buttons and suggests where to put them.
• Several profiles per game (for example driving and on foot): switch them mid-game with SELECT + L1/R1.
• Profiles adapt automatically to screens of other sizes.

EVERY KIND OF CONTROL
• Tap, quick tap, hold, burst and turbo.
• Toggle (one press holds, the next releases) and swipe.
• Timed macros of several touches in a row.
• Virtual joystick with per-stick dead zone and curve, and a D-pad that moves it too.
• Camera on the right stick: sensitivity, inversion, acceleration and aim mode.
• Layers: L2 + A does something different from A alone.
• Gamepad rumble on press and gyroscope camera (no root).
• Two players with two gamepads on the same screen (no root).

COMMUNITY
• Profiles made by other players for your games: see where each button touches before installing it and pick the ones that fit your screen.
• Share your profiles with a link or a QR code, or publish them on the website.
• Built-in profile catalog that works offline.

YOUR GAMEPAD
• Gamepad tester that adapts to what your gamepad has: buttons, sticks, triggers, battery and rumble.
• Generic gamepads in root or Shizuku mode: if your gamepad sends oddly named buttons, the app fixes them with the community mappings and SDL GameControllerDB.

YOUR DATA
• .zip backup and, if you want, a cloud backup with your GitHub account: only you can see it and you can delete it at any time.
• No ads, no purchases and no analytics.

ACCESSIBILITY
The app uses Android's accessibility API only to read your gamepad's buttons and touch the screen at the points you set up. It does not read what you type or the content of other apps, and it does not store or send anything that happens on the screen. It also helps people who can't use touch controls comfortably and prefer to play with a physical gamepad.

Spanish and English. A free personal project by Hades0413, with its source code available on GitHub.
<!-- /completa -->
